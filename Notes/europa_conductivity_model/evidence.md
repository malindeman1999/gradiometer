# Evidence Log for Europa Conductivity Modeling

Date: 2026-02-07
Purpose: Source-backed evidence used in `report.md`.
Framing: Evidence supports physically plausible spatial heterogeneity priors for a static conductivity snapshot used to demonstrate gradiometer capability.

## Core Induction Constraint Papers
1. Khurana et al. (1998), Nature, "Induced magnetic fields as evidence for subsurface oceans in Europa and Callisto".
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/34601
Use: Foundational evidence that Europa requires a conductive subsurface layer.

2. Kivelson et al. (2000), Science, "Galileo Magnetometer Measurements: A Stronger Case for a Subsurface Ocean at Europa".
Link: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.289.5483.1340
Use: Strengthens ocean-induction interpretation with Galileo data.

3. Zimmer et al. (2000), Icarus, "Subsurface Oceans on Europa and Callisto: Constraints from Galileo Magnetometer Observations".
Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019103500963495
Use: Quantitative conductivity-thickness constraints for induced layer.

4. Hand & Chyba (2007), Icarus, "Empirical constraints on the salinity of the europan ocean and implications for a thin ice shell".
Link: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019103507001458
Use: Coupled induction+thickness context; reports conductivity lower-bound discussion in mS/m-to-S/m range.

## Plumes / Exchange Pathways
5. Roth et al. (2014), Science, "Transient Water Vapor at Europa’s South Pole".
Link: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1247051
Use: HST auroral evidence for intermittent plume activity.

6. Sparks et al. (2017), Astrophysical Journal Letters, "Probing for Evidence of Plumes on Europa with HST/STIS".
Link: https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2041-8213/aa67f8
Use: Additional HST plume-candidate detections and recurrence discussion.

7. Jia et al. (2018), Nature Astronomy, "Evidence of a plume on Europa from Galileo magnetic and plasma wave signatures".
Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-018-0450-z
Use: In-situ-like magnetic/plasma consistency with plume encounter scenario.

## Notes on Evidence Strength
- Strongest direct evidence: induced magnetic response and existence of conductive layer.
- Moderate evidence: plume activity and chemistry heterogeneity.
- Indirect but physically grounded: circulation-driven lateral variability in effective conductivity.
